On 2026-10-20 between 00:18 and 00:25 IST, three calls in 8 minutes each ended in "hangup" followed by an immediate redial, and the Pixel's socket dropped (1001 during call 1, 1006 after call 2). The logs could not say who hung up, whether media went peer-to-peer or through the relay, or when and why ICE failed.
