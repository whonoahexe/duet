<assumption_delta_decision>
The assumption-delta probe fired on "pluralization" ("connects peer-to-peer to a second client").
- Primary noun: **connection** (`connId`, one per open WebSocket). A person may have several devices online at once (the owner's phone on cellular plus a laptop for the test), so the hub addresses connections and treats `userId` as an attribute of a connection.
- Decision: **promote**. Rooms hold connection ids, relays go to the other connection in the room, and `closeSession` works per session, not per user. Nothing keyed by `userId` is added alongside.
- Companion invariant test (included in Task 3): two connections of the same user in one room relay only to each other, and a third connection gets `room.full`. Phase 3 binds a call room to the two user ids on top of this; it does not replace the connection identity.
</assumption_delta_decision>
