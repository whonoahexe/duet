## Phase Goal

> ROADMAP Phase 1 Goal (verbatim; not in user-story format): "Both of us can open duet at its own HTTPS address, log in with a password, and install it to our home screens, and the self-hosted SFU is proven to carry media from a phone on cellular."

This plan delivers the "log in with a password" part as one vertical slice: tile, password, shell, logout.

<objective>
Make login real. Tap your name, enter your password, land in the chat-shaped shell (D-11). Stay logged in on a 90-day rolling session (D-06). Log out from the header menu. Abuse controls replace the dropped TOTP (D-05): argon2id, a per-IP-plus-user rate limit keyed on CF-Connecting-IP, an Origin check on state-changing requests, and generic errors.

Purpose: AUTH-02, AUTH-03 and AUTH-04 end-to-end, with the two traps the research reproduced designed out: a rate-limit key that can't see the body (Pitfall 3) and a rolling session that doesn't re-issue the cookie (Pitfall 5).
Output: session and auth modules, guards, three web components, and auth/session test suites. smoke-local.sh is extended with a real login round-trip.
</objective>
