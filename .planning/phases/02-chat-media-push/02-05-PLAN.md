## Phase Goal

> ROADMAP Phase 2 Goal (verbatim, not a user story; no story is invented): "We can message each other in real time, reliably, with history kept forever, share photos, short video clips and voice notes that display and play correctly on both an iPhone and a Pixel (visible only to the two of us), and get a push notification when the app is not open"

This plan completes ROADMAP success criteria 4 (video clips including iPhone HEVC .MOV play on both phones) and 5 (voice notes with a working scrubber, seeking via Range), ships the whole phase, and collects the end-of-phase device UAT.

<objective>
Let either person send short video clips (about 3 minutes / 200 MB) that the server transcodes so both phones can play them (D-08, MDIA-03), and record voice notes by holding or locking the mic that play on both phones with a waveform scrubber (D-10, D-11, D-12, MDIA-04). Then deploy everything and hand the phase to device UAT.

Purpose: the remaining rich-media half of the core value.
Output: probe/video/audio processing lanes, video tiles and viewer playback, recorder and voice-note components, tests, container self-test, final deploy, README media section.
</objective>
