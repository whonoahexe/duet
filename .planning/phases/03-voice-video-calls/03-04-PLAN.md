## Phase Goal

> ROADMAP Phase 3 Goal (verbatim, not a user story; no story is invented): "We can call each other by voice or video, peer-to-peer with Cloudflare TURN relay, on Wi-Fi or cellular, with push-to-answer ringing"

This plan completes ROADMAP success criterion 4 (a brief network drop shows "Reconnecting…" and the call resumes without redialing) and criterion 1's cellular half (relay through Cloudflare TURN), ships the phase and hands it to the device UAT.

<objective>
Make calls survive network blips: rejoin over a new WS connection with stale-connection eviction, ICE restart with rollback of lost offers, one 30 s give-up with "Call dropped" + Call back, a poor-connection badge, and re-acquiring iOS capture tracks on resume. Prove relay-only calls through Cloudflare TURN, deploy, and collect the iPhone/Pixel device UAT.

Purpose: CALL-07 and CALL-09, plus D-17, D-18 (resume), D-20.
Output: call.rejoin/call.state protocol, registry rejoin, realtime live/down seams, peer restart helper, reconnect controller, quality badge, E2E scenarios, final deploy, README Calls section, UAT list.
</objective>
