## Phase Goal

> ROADMAP Phase 3 Goal (verbatim, not a user story; no story is invented): "We can call each other by voice or video, peer-to-peer with Cloudflare TURN relay, on Wi-Fi or cellular, with push-to-answer ringing"

This plan completes ROADMAP success criterion 2: with the recipient's app closed an incoming-call push arrives, tapping it or simply opening the app shows the incoming-call screen, and an unanswered or cancelled call replaces the ring with a missed-call notification.

<objective>
Make calls ring a closed app: an incoming-call Web Push on every callee phone that is not already showing the call, ring-on-attach so that opening duet always shows the incoming screen, a missed-call push that replaces the ring when nobody answers, tap routing in the service worker, and the stale-push toast. Then deploy voice calls with push ringing.

Purpose: CALL-03 (push-to-answer) and the notification half of CALL-04 (ringing stops on every phone).
Output: push payload types, call push sender methods, registry push and attach hooks, GET /api/calls/:id, service-worker call branch, client tap routing and toast, tests, E2E scenarios, deploy.
</objective>
