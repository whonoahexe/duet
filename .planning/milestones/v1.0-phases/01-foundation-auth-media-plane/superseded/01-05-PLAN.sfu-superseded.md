## Phase Goal

> ROADMAP Phase 1 Goal (verbatim; not in user-story format): "Both of us can open duet at its own HTTPS address, log in with a password, and install it to our home screens, and the self-hosted SFU is proven to carry media from a phone on cellular."

This plan delivers the final clause, "the self-hosted SFU is proven to carry media from a phone on cellular", and with it the Phase 3 gate.

<objective>
Prove the media plane end-to-end:
1. duet's server mints HS256 join tokens the home SFU accepts. Phase 3 reuses this minter.
2. A hidden, login-gated harness at `/debug/sfu/` uses the owner's `@sideby-me/rtc` SDK to publish and consume media through the SFU, with a forced-relay mode built from an RTCPeerConnection subclass, so no SDK patch is needed.
3. A phone on cellular reports PASS, direct and relay, measured on inbound-rtp bytesReceived.

Purpose: INFR-04 and the public-reachability half of INFR-03. Implements D-12, D-03 (SFU_URL and TURN_HOST from env) and the Pitfall 11 gate metric.
Output: the token minter and debug routes, the join-check script, the harness page, the BuildKit-secret build for the private SDK, and the recorded cellular results.
</objective>

- token claims are verbatim from rtc-types token.ts;
- the SFU verifies with algorithms ['HS256'] and clockTolerance 60, and rejects participant_already_active;
- the SDK fills the join fields from the token payload;

- protocolVersion is 1.
