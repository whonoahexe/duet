## Phase Goal

> ROADMAP Phase 1 Goal (verbatim; not in user-story format, so no story is invented here): "Both of us can open duet at its own HTTPS address, log in with a password, and install it to our home screens, and a peer-to-peer WebRTC call path (Cloudflare TURN relay) is proven to carry media from a phone on cellular"

This plan delivers the second half of the goal: the call path is proven, first headless on this box with the real Cloudflare TURN key, then on real phones with one of them on cellular.

<objective>

Prove it with a headless two-peer smoke in normal and forced-relay mode, make it reachable from the installed app, deploy it, and hand the cellular test on real phones to the end-of-phase UAT with an automated log check (INFR-04).
