Purpose: INFR-01, INFR-02 and SHEL-02. This also completes the walking skeleton's "deployed" leg.
Output: Dockerfile, compose stack, tunnel setup script, public/persistence smoke scripts, README, PWA manifest, icons and service worker. The phase's end-of-phase device UAT is defined in Task 2.
</objective>
