> ROADMAP Phase 3 Goal (verbatim, not a user story; no story is invented): "We can call each other by voice or video, peer-to-peer with Cloudflare TURN relay, on Wi-Fi or cellular, with push-to-answer ringing"

This plan completes ROADMAP success criterion 1 for video and criterion 3: during a call the user can mute, turn the camera on and off, switch front/back camera and hang up, and the screen does not lock.
